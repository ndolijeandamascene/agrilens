Analytical Architecture

Core Intelligence Modules

Comprehensive data processing addressing cultivation practices, harvest yields, post-harvest losses, and trade dynamics.

Productivity & Yield Dynamics

Multi-season yield tracking (kg/ha), production growth indices, and comparative performance rankings across administrative boundaries.

  • Seasonal yield disaggregation across Seasons A, B, and C
  • Year-over-year production growth rates
  • District and provincial productivity benchmarks

Agricultural Practice Adoption

Monitoring technical input adoption including small-scale irrigation, certified seed usage, mineral and organic fertilizers, and mechanization.

  • Gravity, hillside, and marshland irrigation systems
  • Application rates for DAP, Urea, NPK, and agricultural lime
  • Power tiller and tractor mechanization rates

Post-Harvest Loss Assessment

Quantitative breakdown of loss rates along the post-harvest chain to identify critical points for storage and collection interventions.

  • Loss Rate Formulation: total_loss_kg / production_kg × 100
  • Disaggregation by harvest, transport, and storage stages
  • On-farm storage and processing facility utilization

Market & Value Addition

Tracking wholesale and retail commodity pricing across urban and provincial commercial hubs, alongside household sales rates.

  • Price monitoring across Kigali, Musanze, and Huye hubs
  • Commercial sales ratio vs. subsistence consumption
  • Value-addition and primary processing tracking

Threshold Alerts & Synthesis

Deterministic indicator threshold monitoring to highlight severe productivity deficits, elevated loss rates, or lagging input adoption.

  • Automated outlier identification at district level
  • Evidence synthesis briefs for agricultural planners
  • Early warning triggers for post-harvest loss spikes

Statistical Weighting & Methodology

Rigorous computation applying official NISR sampling weights (weighted_sum, weighted_mean) to ensure representative estimators.

  • Sampling weights applied for population-level validity
  • End-to-end data provenance and transformation records
  • Audit traceability from raw questionnaire variables

Operational Utility & Decision Support

Structured to provide actionable evidence across agricultural planning, research, and resource allocation.

Farmers & Cooperatives

Benchmark local plot yields against district averages. Evaluate regional returns on certified seed, organic manure, and small-scale irrigation schemes.

Agricultural Planners (RAB / MINAGRI)

Identify lagging sectors requiring targeted extension support, input subsidy allocation (Smart Nkunganire), and post-harvest storage facilities.

Policy Institutions

Evaluate national progress against NST2 (2024–2029) agricultural transformation targets and Vision 2050 productivity goals.

Development Partners & Researchers

Direct intervention resources to verified productivity deficit zones and evaluate project outcomes using standardized NISR indicators.

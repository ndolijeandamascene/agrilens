Platform Architecture

Core Agricultural Intelligence Modules

Structured to address every dimension of Track 1: from field cultivation and practices to post-harvest storage and markets.

📊

Productivity Intelligence

Seasonal yield benchmarking, multi-year production growth tracking, and district ranking to uncover regional yield potentials.

  • Yield (kg/ha) across Seasons A, B, and C
  • Historical production growth rates
  • District & provincial productivity league tables
💧

Agricultural Practices

Tracking modern farming techniques: small-scale irrigation, improved seed adoption, organic/inorganic fertilizer, and mechanization.

  • Gravity, motorized, and marshland irrigation
  • DAP, Urea, NPK, and lime application
  • Ox plough and tractor mechanization
📦

Post-Harvest Intelligence

Pinpointing loss hotspots across harvesting, transit, storage, and processing to reduce waste and strengthen food security.

  • Loss Rate: total_loss / production × 100
  • Storage facilities & warehouse availability
  • Processing and value-addition rates
🏪

Market Intelligence

Monitoring commodity prices and sales volumes across Kigali and provincial trade hubs, identifying commercialization opportunities.

  • Prices at Kimironko, Nyabugogo, Musanze, Huye
  • Sales rate vs. household consumption ratio
  • Agro-processing & value addition tracking
⚡

Smart Insights & Alerts

Automated rule-based detection for significant yield deficits, unusual loss spikes, and lagging input adoption rates.

  • Early warning risk detection by sector
  • Data-driven narrative summaries
  • Prioritization briefs for agricultural planners
📐

Statistical Rigor & Lineage

Methodological fidelity to NISR's survey weights (weighted_sum, weighted_mean) and 100% auditable data provenance.

  • Representative sampling weights applied
  • Zero variable fabrication policy
  • Audit trail from SAS raw file to dashboard

1. Algorithmic Architecture & Lineage

Algorithm Random Forest Regressor (100 estimators, max_depth=14)
Target Variable calculated_yield_kg_per_ha (Biological Bounds: 10 – 25,000 kg/ha)
Training Data Official NISR Seasonal Agricultural Survey Microdata (SAS 2024A & 2025A)
Sample Size 4,301 validated plots (with expansion sampling weights)
Validation Scheme Temporal Hold-out: Train on SAS 2024 Season A → Evaluate on SAS 2025 Season A
Evaluation Metrics Weighted R²: 0.6577 • MAE: 1,298 kg/ha (+38.5% improvement vs baseline)
Documented Model Limitations
  • Observational Microdata: Reflects farmer-reported survey associations rather than randomized agronomic trials.
  • Unobserved Factors: Soil acidity (pH), local slope gradient, and micro-climate rainfall variations are not recorded on every survey plot.
  • Non-Causal Interpretability: Fertilizer and certified seeds correlate with higher yield, but management skills and soil quality are unmeasured co-factors.

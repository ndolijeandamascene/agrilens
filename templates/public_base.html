AgriLens See Agriculture Through Data

Agricultural intelligence and decision-support platform developed for the 2026 NISR Big Data Hackathon (Track 1). Converting official Seasonal Agricultural Surveys into practical evidence for Rwanda.

Data • Knowledge • Insight • Decision Support

Platform Modules

  • Productivity Gap Intelligence
  • Interactive Rwanda Map (Leaflet)
  • Irrigation & Input Adoption
  • Post-Harvest Loss Analytics
  • Data Lineage & Variable Audit
  • REST API Endpoints

Data Foundations

  • National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda
  • Seasonal Agricultural Survey (SAS 2023–2025)
  • Agriculture Household Survey (AHS 2024)
  • Ministry of Agriculture (MINAGRI)
  • NST2 & Vision 2050 Priorities

Methodology & Standards

Strict adherence to NISR survey weights (weighted_sum, weighted_mean), zero synthetic data fabrication, and verified variable lineage.

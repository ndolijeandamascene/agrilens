ABOUT AGRILENS

Data-Driven Solutions

AgriLens is Rwanda's agricultural intelligence platform, turning reliable data into practical solutions for farmers, planners and policymakers. We combine NISR data with other open sources to deliver insights, tools and recommendations for a more productive, resilient and sustainable agricultural future.

Our Team

We are a team of data scientists, developers, agricultural experts and change makers, working together for a smarter agriculture in Rwanda.

The Challenge

Rwanda faces persistent agricultural challenges: low productivity, soil degradation, post-harvest losses, climate risks and limited access to market opportunities. Decisions are often made with incomplete or outdated information, limiting the potential of our farmers and agri-businesses.

Our Solution

We transform agricultural data into actionable insights and practical recommendations. Using NISR data and other open sources, AgriLens provides real-time maps, analytics and reports that help farmers, planners and policymakers make better decisions.

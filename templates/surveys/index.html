Seasonal Agricultural Survey (SAS) Data System

The National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda (NISR) conducts the Seasonal Agricultural Survey (SAS) across Season A, Season B, and Season C. Combining aerial imagery, digital GPS segment mapping, and objective crop cut measurements, SAS provides the official gold-standard statistics on cultivated area, crop production, and yield across all 30 districts.

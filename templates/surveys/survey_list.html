NISR Survey Framework

The Seasonal Agricultural Survey (SAS) covers all 30 districts of Rwanda across three distinct seasons (A, B, and C). The sample utilizes aerial orthophotos, segment sampling units, and objective GPS plot boundary measurements to deliver statistically defensible estimates of cultivated area, production, and practice adoption.

Sampling Design:

Stratified Area Sampling Frame with probability proportional to size.

Harmonized Series:

SAS 2024 (Season A) and SAS 2025 (Season A) validated microdata.

Lineage Integration:

10,800 validated plot observations ingested via PostgreSQL ETL.

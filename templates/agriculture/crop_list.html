Agricultural Seasons Calendar

NISR survey cycle & planting calendar

Season A (September – February)

Main rainy season (Itumba). Critical planting window for maize, climbing beans, Irish potato, and soybean across all 30 districts.

Season B (March – June)

Secondary rainy season (Urugaryi). Major harvest season for bush beans, sorghum, cassava, and hillside horticulture.

Season C (July – September)

Dry season (Icyi). Cultivation confined to developed marshlands, irrigated perimeters, and valley bottoms (e.g. rice, market vegetables).
